Build your 2027 USTA teams in under 60 seconds of your time.

Every fall I chase down who wants to play USTA, at what level, and who will captain. This year I sent one email from ClubMode and let it collect the answers. Here is exactly what it did.

1I set it up and sent it

I picked the leagues and whether each one plays days or nights and weekends, set the reply-by date (Oct 30) and the date USTA registration opens (Nov 3). Then Preview, send myself a test, and send. ClubMode pulled every adult member from our roster. Couples who share an inbox each get their own email and their own link.

It also gives me a public link and paste-ready text for the club newsletter, so people who don't read email still land in the same count.

2Members answer in about a minute

One tap from the email opens a short form on their phone:

  • Do you want to play this season? (“Not this season” counts too)
  • Which teams, and day or nights/weekends
  • Their own NTRP level
  • Do they want to captain
  • Confirm they're 40 or older in 2027, for the 40+ leagues
  • When their USTA membership expires

If their USTA membership runs out before the season ends, the confirmation email includes the renewal link automatically.

3I watch the head count fill in

Every answer lands on one screen, grouped by league, level and day or night. I can see who volunteered to captain, who still needs to renew USTA, and anyone from the newsletter link we couldn't match to a member. Export to CSV whenever I want it.

4One click makes the team

When a group has enough players, I click once and ClubMode creates the team in CaptainMode with the roster loaded and the captain noted. From there the captain runs lineups, availability and match emails. CaptainMode is free for captains.
